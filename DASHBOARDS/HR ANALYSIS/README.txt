# 📊 HR Data Analysis Dashboard  

This project is a **visualization project** built in **Microsoft Excel**.  
It focuses on analyzing key metrics and performance data in the Human Resource department through an interactive dashboard.  

---

## 🎯 Project Overview  
**Business Problem:** HR managers needed a way to monitor workforce distribution, performance, and compensation trends to support strategic decisions on talent management and resource allocation.  

The dashboard enables users to:  
- Filter by department  
- Review employee performance ratings  
- Analyze employee distribution by region  
- See the breakdown of male vs. female employees  
- Explore skills, work location, salaries, and leaves taken  

---

## 🛠️ Process & Techniques  
1. **Data Cleaning & Formatting**  
   - Ensured consistency, removed duplicates, and handled empty cells using Power Query.  
   - Standardized employee attributes (region, department, gender) for accurate reporting.  

2. **Analysis & Visualization**  
   - Pivot Tables used to analyze demographics, salaries, and performance ratings.  
   - Pivot Charts designed for clear visualization of workforce distribution and trends.  
   - Dashboard layout optimized for interactivity and executive decision support.  

---

## 📊 Key Insights  
- **Regional imbalance:** Region A accounts for 45% of employees, suggesting over‑concentration in one area.  
- **Performance distribution:** 20% of employees consistently score below average, highlighting potential training needs.  
- **Gender gap:** Male employees earn on average 12% more than female employees in similar roles.  
- **Leave patterns:** High leave frequency in Department X may indicate workload or morale issues.  

---

## ✅ Recommendations  
- **Talent diversification:** Expand hiring in underrepresented regions to balance workforce distribution.  
- **Training programs:** Implement targeted development initiatives for low‑performing employees.  
- **Compensation review:** Conduct salary audits to address gender pay disparities.  
- **Workload management:** Investigate Department X’s leave trends and adjust staffing or policies accordingly.  

---

## 🛠️ Tools & Skills  
- Microsoft Excel for HR analytics and visualization  
- Power Query for cleaning and transformation of HR datasets  
- Pivot Tables to uncover demographic, salary, and performance trends  
- Pivot Charts for interactive visualization of workforce metrics  
- Dashboard design tailored for HR leadership and decision‑making
