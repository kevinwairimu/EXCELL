# ☕ Coffee Orders Data Analysis Dashboard  

This project is a **visualization project** built in **Microsoft Excel**.  
It focuses on analyzing coffee sales data across multiple metrics and presenting insights through an interactive dashboard.  

---

## 🎯 Project Overview  
**Business Problem:** Coffee distributors needed a way to quickly identify revenue drivers, customer concentration risks, and product performance across regions.  

The dashboard enables users to:  
- Track sales trends over time  
- Analyze sales by country  
- Identify the top 5 customers and their revenue share  
- Review total sales per coffee type  
- Explore revenue generated across different product categories  

---

## 🛠️ Process & Techniques  
1. **Data Gathering**  
   - Customer information (name, email, country) dynamically retrieved using `INDEX MATCH` from `customer.xlsx`.  
   - Product information (coffee type, roast type, size, unit price) dynamically retrieved using `INDEX MATCH` from `product.xlsx`.  
   - Sales calculated using `Unit Price × Quantity`.  
   - Coffee type and roast type formatted into full names.  
   - Date formatted consistently as `dd/mmm/yy`.  

2. **Analysis & Visualization**  
   - Timeline analysis of total sales.  
   - Sales breakdown by country.  
   - Ranking of top customers.  
   - Sales distribution per coffee type.  
   - Revenue visualization across categories.  

---

## 📊 Key Insights  
- The top 5 customers contribute over 30% of total revenue, highlighting dependency risk.  
- Ireland & united kingdom are reporting average sales, suggesting untapped market potential or distribution challenges. 
- Robosta coffee generates the lowest revenue share, indicating weak consumer preference, while Liberia & excelica generates the highest revenue 
- Dark roast coffee generates the highest revenue share, indicating weak consumer preference.  
- 2.5 kg coffee size generates the highest revenue share, indicating strong consumer preference. 
- Between March & september the sales are high

---

## ✅ Recommendations  
- Diversify customer base to reduce reliance on a small group of high‑value clients.  
- Target marketing and distribution efforts in underperforming countries to capture growth opportunities.  
- Expand product focus on dark roast varieties while monitoring emerging trends in other categories.
- Stocking 2.5 kg size more in the shops

---

## 🛠️ Tools & Skills  
- Microsoft Excel for end‑to‑end analysis  
- `INDEX MATCH` for dynamic lookups across multiple datasets  
- Pivot Tables to uncover country‑level disparities and customer concentration  
- Pivot Charts for trend visualization and revenue breakdowns  
- Dashboard design for executive‑level decision support
